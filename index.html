About Us

Vision, Mission & Objectives

What HCS stands for, and what guides every exhibition, auction and workshop we run.

Vision

To make CET home to Kerala's most active student-run numismatic and antique-collecting community — a space where heritage is exhibited, traded fairly, and passed on with knowledge.

Mission

  • Unite collectors of coins, currency, stamps and antiques from every department and year.
  • Facilitate safe, transparent exchange and auctioning through curated events and our website.
  • Educate members and the wider student body through workshops on numismatics, finance and trade.
  • Celebrate outstanding collections through judged exhibitions and inter-club competitions.
  • Sustain the club through modest, transparent service charges rather than external funding alone.

Objectives

  • A recognised platform for collectors to showcase and grow their collections.
  • A website for listing, discovering and exchanging collectibles across campus.
  • Periodic exhibitions with structured judging that rewards quality collecting.
  • Fair, transparent auctions with a clear commission structure.
  • Workshops building financial literacy, numismatic knowledge and trade skills.
  • Joint fairs with other departments and clubs for wider engagement.
  • Sustainable revenue through minimal, fair service charges on club activities.
